West Texas Leaf & Pest Troubleshooter

Quick Customer Fix
Yellow Leaves + Dark Green Veins Iron Chlorosis

Cause: High-pH alkaline Lubbock soil locks up iron (common on Red Oaks, Photinias, Hollies).

Fix: Apply Chelated Iron (EDDHA) + Soil Sulfur, and avoid overwatering.

Yellow/Mushy Leaves Dropping on Sage Overwatering / Root Rot

Cause: Drip irrigation running too often in heavy clay soil on Cenizo, Rosemary, or Lavender.

Fix: Turn off emitter for 2–3 weeks; let soil dry completely between deep soaks.

Fine Webbing & Speckled Dry Leaves Spider Mites

Cause: Hot, dry, dusty West Texas wind (loves Junipers, Roses, Boxwoods, Tomatoes).

Fix: Blast undersides of leaves with water hose + spray Insecticidal Soap or Neem Oil at dusk.

Crispy Brown Leaf Edges on Shade Plants Afternoon Sun / Wind Scorch

Cause: Hot west sun or 30+ mph winds drying foliage faster than roots can pull water.

Fix: Add 3" hardwood mulch over roots, deep-water at base, or move to east/north exposure.
